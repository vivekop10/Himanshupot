About Me

I'm Himanshu Rajput — a multidisciplinary graphic designer and visual artist based in India, specializing in print media, social media design, motion graphics, and 3D visualization.

With a strong foundation from MAAC AGRA in high-end 3D Animation, VFX & Graphic Design, I bring a unique blend of artistic vision and technical precision to every project. From editorial layouts and book design to dynamic social media content, I create visuals that captivate and communicate.
